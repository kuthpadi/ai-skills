# Anti-Patterns

## Generic Corporate Language

### Avoid

- Significant improvement
- Meaningful impact
- Operational excellence
- Strategic initiatives
- Actionable insights
- Innovative solutions
- Transformational capabilities

### Prefer

Concrete business outcomes and measurable results.

---

## Buzzword Inflation

### Avoid

- AI-powered everything
- Excessive GenAI references
- Architecture jargon stacking
- Trendy terminology without outcomes

### Prefer

Business impact, adoption, and operational enablement.

---

## Weak Ownership

### Avoid

Contributed to various initiatives.

Supported multiple projects.

Worked on analytics efforts.

### Prefer

Led, Enabled, Developed, Established, Scaled.

Use the strongest credible ownership signal.

---

## Project Inventory Resume

### Avoid

Project 1

Project 2

Project 3

Project 4

### Prefer

A leadership progression narrative showing increasing scope, scale, and business impact.

---

## Metric Stuffing

### Avoid

Every bullet contains a percentage.

### Prefer

Selective high-value quantification tied to business outcomes.

---

## Tool Dumping

### Avoid

Python, SQL, Spark, AWS, Databricks, Airflow, Kafka, TensorFlow...

### Prefer

Platform leadership, business enablement, and enterprise outcomes.

---

## Technical Detail Overload

### Avoid

Excessive implementation details.

Algorithm selection discussions.

Low-level engineering mechanics.

### Prefer

Operational outcomes and organizational impact.

---

## Overstated Attribution

### Avoid

Claiming ownership of outcomes that were organizational or team achievements.

### Prefer

Accurate attribution that remains credible under scrutiny.

---

## Excessive Synonym Optimization

### Avoid

Spearheaded

Orchestrated

Championed

Revolutionized

Pioneered

### Prefer

Led

Leading

Drove

Developed

Established

Scaled

Enabled

---

## AI Resume Trap

### Avoid

Stacking AI buzzwords without evidence of adoption, governance, scale, or business impact.

### Prefer

Demonstrating operationalized AI through measurable outcomes.
